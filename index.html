• Seed: A small, smiling yellow/golden ball who just wants his cookies and appears in obstacle, rolling, and merging animations. He also has forms and variations—such as metal and dimension seeds—and hybrids like the Seed-Kevin Hybrid. • Kevin (or Kelvin): A dark/black circular character who repeatedly tries to stop, torture, or get rid of Seed. • Nokia: A Nokia 3310 model featured alongside Seed and Kevin in simulation and merge-style videos
